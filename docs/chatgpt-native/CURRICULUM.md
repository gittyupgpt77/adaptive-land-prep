# Adaptive Land Prep — authoritative curriculum

## Purpose
This file is the durable curriculum source for the ChatGPT-native BUD/S land-preparation system. The archived PWA implementation is provenance, not the active coach. Swimming remains outside this 56-week land curriculum unless the user explicitly changes scope. Prefer Concept2 rowing when impact is unnecessary; retain running because running-specific tissue adaptation matters.

## Governing rules
- Weeks are curriculum position, not permission to force volume.
- Weekly run figures are ceilings/guardrails, not daily prescriptions or quotas.
- Ruck figures are weekly/long-session guidance, not permission to jump pack load.
- Increase distance before pack load and preserve normal gait.
- Hard running/rowing intensity requires an established pace, HR or lactate calibration. If none is current, do not invent intervals; substitute easy Concept2 work within that week's rowing allowance.
- A calendar week may advance while the earned prescription is held at a phase boundary until exit criteria are met.
- Missed/unresolved days do not get “made up” by cramming volume.
- The system may reduce work below the planned curriculum based on recovery/mechanical/fueling rules. It may not exceed the curriculum merely because recovery looks good.

## Five macro phases
- **Phase 1: Foundation — Weeks 1–16**: Lose excess body fat, rebuild the aerobic base, establish strength consistency and prepare tissues for impact.
- **Phase 2: Engine + Load — Weeks 17–32**: Build running speed, threshold capacity and loaded-walking durability.
- **Phase 3: Specificity — Weeks 33–44**: Shift resources toward selection-specific running, bodyweight performance and loaded movement.
- **Phase 4: Peak Work Capacity — Weeks 45–52**: Demonstrate high accumulated workload without localized bone pain or mechanical breakdown.
- **Phase 5: Taper — Weeks 53–56**: Dissipate fatigue, retain key qualities and arrive healthy.

## Strength anchors
**Session A**
- Back squat: 3 × 5
- Romanian deadlift: 3 × 6–8
- Overhead press: 3 × 5
- Pull-ups: 3–5 submaximal sets
- Tibialis raises: 3 × 15–25
- Calf/soleus raises: 3 × 12–20
- Neck harness: 2–3 controlled sets each direction
- Grip work: 2–3 sets

**Session B**
- Split squat or reverse lunge: 3 × 6–8 each leg
- Hip hinge: 3 × 6–8
- Horizontal press + row: 3 × 6–10 each
- Suitcase carry: 3 × 40–100 m each side
- Tibialis + soleus: 3 sets each
- Neck harness: 2–3 controlled sets
- Forearm roller: 2–3 climbs

Strength progression is history-informed and conservative. Do not automatically raise load. Comparable pain-free sessions can support a suggestion to increase, not an automatic change.

## Phase exit criteria
**Foundation (Weeks 1–16)**
- 7-day operating rhythm: at least 7 check-ins
- 8 strength sessions
- 8 aerobic sessions
- 14 complete days
- No arbitrary body-fat percentage is a promotion lock.

**Engine + Load (Weeks 17–32)**
- 28 complete days
- 12 loaded-work sessions
- 16 aerobic sessions
- No unresolved curriculum debt
- 4 miles under 24:00
- 12 miles with 30 lb
- Loaded walking feels comfortable
- Phase mastery at least 75%

**Specificity (Weeks 33–44)**
Qualification set 1:
- 80 strict push-ups
- 15 strict pull-ups
- 70 sit-ups
- Phase mastery at least 75%

Exit set:
- 100 strict push-ups
- 20 strict pull-ups
- 90 sit-ups
- 4 miles in 22:00 or faster

**Peak Work Capacity (Weeks 45–52)**
- 14 complete phase days
- 8 loaded/endurance sessions
- Phase mastery at least 75%
- No unresolved curriculum debt
- Peak simulation completed pain-free
- 100 strict push-ups
- 20 strict pull-ups
- 4 miles at or under 22:00

**Taper (Weeks 53–56)**
- 14 complete taper days
- No unresolved curriculum debt
- Peak durability remains pain-free
- Maintain selection benchmark: 4 miles ≤22:00, 100 push-ups, 20 pull-ups
- Mechanical check-in clear
- Nutrition day complete
- Final check-in complete
- Program mastery at least 80%

## Program accounting
A complete day requires both a check-in and a completed session. Phase mastery is based on completed days across the phase, with failed days penalized. Unresolved missed days block promotion. The ChatGPT-native system should keep this accounting in the private CURRENT_STATE file rather than relying on Memory.
